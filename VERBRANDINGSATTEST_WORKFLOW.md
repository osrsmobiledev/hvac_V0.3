Last updated: 2026-10-21

## Purpose

Preserve the owner's tab-by-tab explanation of the old Eurosoft - Attest applet and the intended changes for the new Flutter app. Update this document as the explanation continues so it remains available across chats.

This document describes product behavior. It does not select technical architecture or establish current regulatory requirements. Read PROJECT_BRIEF.md for the broader product requirements and collaboration preferences.

## Tab 1 — Data

### Old app: confirmed behavior

- Displays the first flue-gas measurement data taken using the Eurolyzer measurement instrument.
- The instrument connects to the old app using Bluetooth.
- The owner confirmed that the emulator is running a demo that simulates a connected instrument. Displayed measurement values are demo data, not actual boiler readings.

### New app: confirmed change

- Obtain the measurement data by scanning a QR code generated by the Eurolyzer instrument, replacing the Bluetooth measurement-transfer workflow.
- The QR payload format, supported instrument models, and parsing rules have not yet been supplied or verified.

### Screenshot observations

Reference folder: [Data screenshots](references/legacy/screenshots/Data/).

- The tab contains an expandable **Measurement Data** section and a nested **Fluegas** section.
- Visible readings include fuel, date, time, pressure, temperatures, O2, CO, NO, SO2, CO2, efficiency Hi/Hs, excess air, losses, referenced CO, dew point, and boiler temperature.
- Different units are visible, including ppm and mg/kWh for CO-related values. Their exact meanings and conversions must be established before mapping QR values to certificate fields.
- Values in the screenshots are examples, not default values, acceptance limits, or validated measurements.

### Open questions

- Resolved by direct inspection: Meting displays a changing instrument-data panel; **Invoegen** explicitly captures readings into the selected initial/final measurement slot. See the Meting section below.
- How should a QR scan be initiated in the new app, and what should happen after a successful scan?
- How are additional measurements, rescans, missing readings, and corrections handled? Discuss these after the owner explains the normal flow.

## Tab 2 — Start

### Owner-confirmed behavior

- After Data, the technician taps the **Start** tab to switch to it.
- Select one of two options: **Onderhoud** or **Keuring voor eerste ingebruikname**.
- Enter the certificate number (**Volgnummer Attest**).
- These are the functions described for this tab.

Reference folder: [Start screenshots](references/legacy/screenshots/Start/).

### Details not yet decided

- Certificate-number format, uniqueness scope, and whether the new app offers automatic numbering have not been specified. The described workflow uses manual entry; do not silently replace it with automatic numbering.

## Shared certificate data — confirmed requirement for the new app

- Retain the extracted Data measurements and the information entered in Start as part of the same maintenance/certificate record.
- Switching tabs must preserve the collected information; values must not exist only in the currently displayed screen.
- This retained information will later be used to generate the PDF and the required VEKA JSON payload.
- In line with the app's confirmed offline requirement, the draft must be stored locally. The exact storage implementation and save timing remain to be designed.
- Use the saved record as the source for output generation. PDF and VEKA mappings may require different subsets or representations of the information; their exact fields must be established from the relevant templates and API documentation.
- Direct inspection established that the old app's Meting uses **Invoegen** to capture live readings into a selected measurement slot. The exact QR-to-slot interaction for the new app remains to be agreed.

## Tab 3 — Firma – Technicus

### Owner-confirmed behavior

- Tap **Firma – Technicus** to open the tab.
- The technician fills in the company details and their personal technician information.
- Enter the maintenance date and the work start and end times.

Reference folder: [Firma screenshots](references/legacy/screenshots/Firma/).

### Fields visible in the screenshots

- Company: name, street, number, postcode, municipality, telephone, email, and enterprise number.
- Technician: first name, last name, and recognized-technician identifiers labeled **GV** and **TV**.
- Visit: **Datum onderhoud**, **Arbeidsduur van**, and **tot**, with date/time picker controls.
- These labels are observed in the old UI. Their requiredness and validation rules have not yet been established.

### New app: owner preferences

- Support swiping between tabs as well as tapping them. Exact gesture behavior remains to be agreed; the owner expressed this as a preference.
- When a new applet/certificate draft opens, preselect the maintenance date to the current local date.
- The maintenance date remains editable, as confirmed by the owner.
- Keep this date with the saved certificate draft. Opening an existing draft must not silently replace its saved date with today's date.
- Company, technician, date, and time information belong to the same saved record as the other tabs and are retained for later outputs.
- Store company and technician details persistently for reuse in future certificates. Completing a task or clearing/resetting the applet must not erase these details; they remain until the technician explicitly edits them.
- Treat reusable company/technician details separately from visit-specific date and times. Clearing a visit must not clear the reusable details.

### Implementation recommendation — not yet confirmed

- Preserve the company/technician details used for each completed certificate so later profile edits do not silently change historical certificates. The exact snapshot/finalization behavior remains to be designed.

### Still unspecified

- Start/end-time defaults have not been requested. Do not automatically invent work times.
- Whether an explicit profile edit also updates an already-open draft remains undecided.

- All visible classification/fuel radio options and parent checkboxes were exercised. Text fields were inventoried; exhaustive input validation, unit validation, and invalid-number tests were not performed.

- No actual boiler details were entered and no certificate was finalized. Classification/fuel selections were returned to unchecked at the end; the tab remains scrolled to the appliance/burner fields.

### Data-loss behavior observed

- After capturing initial gas readings and setting some assessments, switching Meting to solid blanked and disabled the gas/liquid numeric readings and cleared related assessments.
- Reselecting gas did not restore the initial captured readings; the slot remained blank. This is evidence of lost displayed/captured state in the tested sequence, not merely hidden values.
- Common safety selections for flue pressure and ventilation remained across those fuel changes. Inapplicable flue/supply-tightness selections were cleared.
- No warning about losing captured readings was observed during the fuel switches.

### Recommendations for the replacement app — awaiting agreement

- Choose the installation's fuel once in Stooktoestel and derive measurement applicability from that saved choice. Meting can display the fuel without requiring an independent duplicate selection.
- Preserve the fuel reported by the instrument/QR separately from the installation classification. Flag a mismatch for review rather than silently changing one to match the other.
- Keep imported source readings with their units and capture context, and explicitly assign each capture to an initial, final-low-load, or final-high-load slot.
- Changing the appliance fuel must not silently erase imported source readings. Define how incompatible assigned values are preserved, flagged, and excluded from final outputs before coding this behavior.
- Keep measurement data and technician assessments distinct. Do not automatically certify safety from demo values or guessed thresholds.
- Confirm the actual QR payload and measurement units before designing conversions or mapping values into PDF/VEKA outputs.

### Test limits and state left in the emulator

- **Verwijderen** was not activated. Deletion behavior remains unverified. No PDF, email, or VEKA submission was performed.
- Manual numeric edits, insertion over an already populated slot, cross-slot assessment ownership, and persistence across restarts were not tested.
- Stooktoestel was left with gas selected. Meting was later changed to liquid for comparison and is currently scrolled near Rookindex; this mismatch is a test artifact.
- Synthetic operating/safety selections remain in the demo draft. The captured initial numeric readings were cleared by the tested solid-fuel transition. None of these constitute an actual technician assessment.

## Online requirements review and proposed field visibility — 2026-10-21

### Sources and responsibilities

- [Official technician guidance and model documents](https://www.vlaanderen.be/natuur-milieu-en-klimaat/informatie-voor-de-technicus-vloeibare-en-technicus-gasvormige-brandstof-cv-ketel): official models contain minimum certificate content; personalization and additions are allowed, shortening is not. The page links a model-verbrandingsattest named Verbrandingsattest_hvevpk.docx, matching the supplied filename. The remote DOCX was not parsed by the browser tool, so byte identity with the supplied file has not been verified.
- [Official maintenance guidance](https://www.vlaanderen.be/verplicht-onderhoud-van-uw-cv-installatie-centrale-verwarming): fuel and power affect maintenance obligations and document requirements. Do not apply one interval/document set to all installations. The guidance distinguishes solid-fuel documentation from gas/oil, so the precise solid-fuel applet/output scope needs validation before production.
- [Official combustion requirements](https://www.vlaanderen.be/bouwen-wonen-en-energie/verwarming-koeling-en-ventilatie/keuring-en-onderhoud-van-uw-verwarming/eisen-verbrandingsrendement-van-cv-ketel) and its linked [combustion-values sheet](https://assets.vlaanderen.be/image/upload/v1647524890/Verbrandingswaarden_centrale_stooktoestellen_mbuj5y.pdf): distinguish gas B1 versus non-B1 and LPG; fuel category alone is insufficient for every threshold. The sheet states that O2, CO2, and net temperature still need measuring/recording despite no longer having their own normative thresholds. Do not hide them merely because no pass/fail limit applies.
- [VEKA Meldingen API](https://vo-veka.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/TDV/pages/2294841459/Meldingen+API) and [submission method](https://vo-veka.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/TDV/pages/2400387096/meldingCentraleStooktoestellen): govern reporting access and payloads, not the entire measurement UI or certificate content.

The new app should share a consistent saved installation/visit record, with separate applicability/validation and output mappings for the form, certificate, and VEKA payload. A field omitted from the JSON example is not automatically unnecessary in the form or PDF.

### Proposed form behavior

The owner proposed making irrelevant fields disappear instead of leaving them grey. Recommendation: hide fields only when established requirements make them inapplicable; keep missing required inputs visible.
